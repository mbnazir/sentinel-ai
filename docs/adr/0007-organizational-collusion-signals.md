# ADR 0007: Collusion is a relationship risk signal, not an accusation

Status: Accepted

Sentinel may score repeated relationships such as supervisor-agent or manager-agent interventions, but must not label collusion solely from statistical association. A collusion investigation requires deterministic underlying evidence, recurrence across shift dates, and sufficient population/sample size. Missing hierarchy is reported as data quality rather than inferred. Payroll actor identity is currently unavailable in Quartz activity records and therefore payroll-user attribution is prohibited.
